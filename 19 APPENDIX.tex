\section{Appendix}\label{sec:appendix}

This appendix collects the quantitative tearsheets and figure-level diagnostics that accompany the empirical results of Section~\ref{sec:result}. To keep the page count manageable I report each figure-level diagnostic for the \emph{August 2024} episode only, across all three pair constructions; the corresponding December 2024 and April 2025 figures, together with all raw outputs, daily tables, and plotting scripts, are available in the open-source companion repository \parencite{furnes2026repo}. The reader is encouraged to consult the repository directly for the full grid of results across the three monthly episodes and the three pair constructions, and for any additional diagnostics not reproduced here.

The appendix is organised as follows. The three per-pair tearsheets (Section~\ref{sec:apx-tearsheets}) collect return, risk, risk-adjusted, trading, and distribution diagnostics across all three monthly episodes on a single page each. The figure-grid sections that follow are arranged so that one plot type occupies one page where possible: cross-pair cumulative-PnL panels on a symmetric-log axis (Section~\ref{sec:apx-strategy-panels}), rolling spread volatility (Section~\ref{sec:apx-rolling-vol}), per-bar period returns (Section~\ref{sec:apx-period-returns}), spread-return distributions for the two pairs not shown in the body (Section~\ref{sec:apx-return-distributions}), and per-day HMM regime dynamics (Section~\ref{sec:apx-markov-dynamics}). The daily strategy summary tables for all three pairs across all three months close the appendix.
