\section{Appendix}\label{sec:appendix}

This appendix collects the figure-level diagnostics that accompany the empirical results of Section~\ref{sec:result}. To keep the page count manageable we report each diagnostic for the \emph{August 2024} episode only, across all three pair constructions; the corresponding December 2024 and April 2025 figures, together with all raw outputs, daily tables, and plotting scripts, are available in the open-source companion repository \parencite{furnes2026repo}. The reader is encouraged to consult the repository directly for the full grid of results across the three monthly episodes and the three pair constructions, and for all additional diagnostics not reproduced here.
