\section{Appendix}\label{sec:appendix}

This appendix collects the quantitative tearsheets, figure-level diagnostics, and raw daily ledgers that accompany the empirical results of Section~\ref{sec:result}. Where the body of the thesis presents headline numbers and a small number of representative figures, the appendix is the place to look for the full back-test grid, the per-pair regime dynamics, and the trade-by-trade behaviour that the prose summarises. To keep the page count manageable we report each figure-level diagnostic for the August 2024 episode only, across all three pair constructions; the corresponding December 2024 and April 2025 figures, together with all raw outputs, daily tables, and plotting scripts, are available in the open-source companion repository \parencite{furnes2026repo}. The reader is encouraged to consult the repository directly for the full grid of results across the three monthly episodes and the three pair constructions, and for any additional diagnostics not reproduced here.

\paragraph{Reading the magnitudes.} Several of the plots below display quantities that look numerically small at first glance, and we want to flag the units explicitly so the reader is not misled into thinking the results are numerical artefacts. Per-bar spread returns $\Delta S_t$ are pre-averaged tick log-returns, which are intrinsically of order $10^{-5}$ to $10^{-4}$ per bar; this is the natural scale of high-frequency FX log-returns and is not a sign of underflow or missing data. Cumulative PnL is reported throughout in basis points ($\text{bps} = 10^{-4}$), and the symlog $y$-axis used in Section~\ref{sec:apx-strategy-panels} compresses both very small and very large excursions onto the same panel by design. The pre-averaging step of Section~\ref{sec:preaveraging} produces a regular grid of pre-averaged blocks, the cointegration estimation winsorises and rescales the spread for numerical stability (Section~\ref{sec:msarspread}), and rows with missing bid/ask quotes are dropped before any model is fitted, so the apparent ``small numbers'' in the late plots reflect the bar-level scale of FX tick data rather than null values or floating-point underflow.

The appendix is organised in three blocks. The first block is the three per-pair tearsheets in Section~\ref{sec:apx-tearsheets}, one table per pair, stacking the three monthly episodes vertically so that return, risk, risk-adjusted performance, trading activity, and distributional diagnostics can be read side by side for the four strategies on a single page. The second block is a sequence of figure grids, with one plot type per page where possible: cross-pair cumulative-PnL panels on a symmetric-log axis in Section~\ref{sec:apx-strategy-panels} (the same view of Figure~\ref{fig:audnzd-3m} for the two pairs not shown in the body), rolling spread volatility in Section~\ref{sec:apx-rolling-vol}, per-bar period returns in Section~\ref{sec:apx-period-returns}, spread-return distributions for the two remaining pairs in Section~\ref{sec:apx-return-distributions}, and per-day HMM regime dynamics in Section~\ref{sec:apx-markov-dynamics}. The third block is the daily strategy summary tables of Section~\ref{sec:apx-daily-tables}, one per pair, reporting every trading day in every episode at the round-trip level so that the bleed pattern of the Baseline and the gating behaviour of AR and MS can be inspected directly rather than only in aggregate. The appendix closes with a symbol dictionary (Section~\ref{sec:apx-symbols}) intended as a lookup reference for the notation used in the methods sections.
