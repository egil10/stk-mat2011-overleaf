\section{Appendix}\label{sec:appendix}

This appendix collects the quantitative tearsheets, figure-level diagnostics, and raw daily ledgers that go with the empirical results of Section~\ref{sec:result}. The body presents headline numbers and a small number of representative figures; the appendix is where the full back-test grid, the per-pair regime dynamics, and the trade-by-trade behaviour live. To keep the page count manageable we show each figure-level diagnostic for the August 2024 episode only, across all three pair constructions. The corresponding December 2024 and April 2025 figures, together with all raw outputs, daily tables, and plotting scripts, are in the open-source companion repository \parencite{furnes2026repo}.

\paragraph{Reading the magnitudes.} Several of the plots below show quantities that look numerically small at first glance, and we flag the units here so they are not misread as numerical artefacts. Per-bar spread returns $\Delta S_t$ are pre-averaged tick log-returns; on FX tick data they are naturally of order $10^{-5}$ to $10^{-4}$ per bar. That is the scale of high-frequency log-returns, not a sign of underflow or missing data. Cumulative PnL is reported in basis points ($\text{bps} = 10^{-4}$), and the symlog $y$-axis used in Section~\ref{sec:apx-strategy-panels} compresses both very small and very large excursions onto the same panel by design. The pre-averaging step of Section~\ref{sec:preaveraging} produces a regular grid of blocks, the cointegration estimation winsorises and rescales the spread for numerical stability (Section~\ref{sec:msarspread}), and rows with missing bid or ask quotes are dropped before any model is fitted. The ``small numbers'' in the late plots therefore reflect the bar-level scale of FX tick data, not null values or floating-point underflow.

The appendix is organised in three blocks. The first block is the per-pair tearsheets in Section~\ref{sec:apx-tearsheets}, one table per pair, stacking the three monthly episodes so that return, risk, risk-adjusted performance, trading activity, and distributional diagnostics can be read side by side for the four strategies on a single page. The second block is a sequence of figure grids, one plot type per page where possible: cross-pair cumulative-PnL panels on a symmetric-log axis in Section~\ref{sec:apx-strategy-panels} (the same view of Figure~\ref{fig:audnzd-3m} for the two pairs not shown in the body), rolling spread volatility in Section~\ref{sec:apx-rolling-vol}, per-bar period returns in Section~\ref{sec:apx-period-returns}, spread-return distributions for the two remaining pairs in Section~\ref{sec:apx-return-distributions}, and per-day HMM regime dynamics in Section~\ref{sec:apx-markov-dynamics}. The third block is the daily strategy summary tables of Section~\ref{sec:apx-daily-tables}, one per pair, reporting every trading day in every episode at the round-trip level so that the bleed pattern of the Baseline and the gating behaviour of AR and MS can be inspected directly. The appendix closes with a symbol dictionary (Section~\ref{sec:apx-symbols}) used as a lookup reference for the notation in the methods sections.
