\section{Back-test protocol and metrics}\label{sec:backtest}

\paragraph{Costs and walk-forward.}\label{sec:walkforward}
Every position change in leg $i$ pays the half-spread $\mathrm{HS}_t^{\,i}$ of \eqref{eq:midprice} plus a fixed commission \parencite{aldridge2013hft,lopezdeprado2018advances}, so the round-trip cost of one pair-position is approximately $\mathrm{HS}^{A} + \hat\beta\,\mathrm{HS}^{B}$ in basis points. Strategies are evaluated walk-forward: parameters $\Theta$ and hyperparameters $(W_\beta, W_z, z_q, z_x, z_v, \delta)$ are fitted on a training segment and applied to a disjoint out-of-sample test segment, with windows slid forward \parencite{lopezdeprado2018advances,hautsch2012econometrics}.

\paragraph{Performance metrics.}\label{sec:metrics}
Let $R_t$ denote the strategy return at bar $t$, $\mu_R = \mathbb{E}[R]$, $\sigma_R = \sqrt{\mathrm{Var}(R)}$, $V_t$ the portfolio value with running peak $V_t^{\star} = \max_{s \leq t} V_s$, and $R_f$ a benchmark risk-free rate (set to zero). Statistical metrics describe the shape of the return distribution; financial metrics translate it into capital outcomes \parencite{tsay2010,cont2001empirical}. The annualised volatility, skewness, and excess kurtosis are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:statmetrics}
    \sigma_{\mathrm{ann}} \;=\; \sigma_R\sqrt{T},
    \qquad
    \mathcal{S} \;=\; \frac{\mathbb{E}[(R-\mu_R)^{3}]}{\sigma_R^{3}},
    \qquad
    \mathcal{K} \;=\; \frac{\mathbb{E}[(R-\mu_R)^{4}]}{\sigma_R^{4}}.
\end{equation}
With $\alpha = 0.95$, the Value-at-Risk, Conditional VaR, and tail-fatness ratio are \parencite{artzner1999coherent}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:varcvar}
    \mathrm{VaR}_\alpha \;=\; -Q_{1-\alpha}(R),
    \qquad
    \mathrm{CVaR}_\alpha \;=\; -\,\mathbb{E}\bigl[R \mid R \leq -\mathrm{VaR}_\alpha\bigr],
    \qquad
    \mathrm{TF} \;=\; \frac{\mathrm{CVaR}_\alpha}{\mathrm{VaR}_\alpha}.
\end{equation}
The drawdown family uses $D_t = (V_t^{\star} - V_t)/V_t^{\star}$, with maximum drawdown and Ulcer index
\begin{equation}\label{eq:drawdown}
    \mathrm{MDD} \;=\; \max_{t}\, D_t,
    \qquad
    \mathrm{UI} \;=\; \sqrt{\,\frac{1}{N}\sum_{t=1}^{N} D_t^{2}\,},
\end{equation}
where $\mathrm{UI}$ is the Ulcer index of \textcite{martin1989ulcer} and the maximum drawdown is treated in \textcite{magdonismail2004drawdown}. Risk-adjusted ratios relate excess return to risk: the Sharpe ratio \parencite{sharpe1994ratio} divides by total volatility, the Sortino ratio \parencite{sortino1991downside} divides by downside deviation $\sigma_d$, and the Calmar ratio divides annualised return $\mathrm{AR}$ by the worst observed drawdown,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ratios}
    \mathrm{SR} \;=\; \frac{\mu_R - R_f}{\sigma_R},
    \qquad
    \mathrm{Sortino} \;=\; \frac{\mu_R - R_f}{\sigma_d},
    \qquad
    \mathrm{Calmar} \;=\; \frac{\mathrm{AR}}{\mathrm{MDD}}.
\end{equation}
Trade-level diagnostics (win rate, market exposure, profit factor, payoff ratio) summarise frequency and size; see Section~\ref{sec:result} for their interpretation.
