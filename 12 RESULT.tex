\subsubsection{EURNOK/EURSEK}

For EURNOK/EURSEK, the strategy fails economically across all three model classes. The AR and MS-AR filters reduce exposure, volatility, and total losses, but they do not convert the pair into a profitable trading signal. This suggests that the pair contains intermittent statistical structure, but not enough exploitable mean reversion after spread costs and adaptive thresholding.

\subsection{Summary of results}

The HMM does not consistently outperform simpler trading rules. Instead, it mainly reduces exposure and volatility. This is beneficial in the failing EURNOK/EURSEK case, but costly in the profitable AUDUSD/NZDUSD and GBPUSD/EURUSD cases, where the baseline or AR strategies preserve more upside.
