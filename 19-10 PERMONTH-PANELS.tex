\subsection{Per-month symlog strategy panels}\label{sec:apx-permonth-panels}

Single-month four-strategy cumulative-PnL panels on a symmetric-log $y$-axis. Rows index the pair construction (AUD/USD--NZD/USD, EUR/NOK--EUR/SEK, GBP/USD--EUR/USD); columns index the monthly episode (August 2024, December 2024, April 2025). The symlog scale preserves the shape of the small-amplitude AR and MS-AR curves alongside the large Baseline drawdowns.
